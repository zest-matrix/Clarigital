AI Voice Agents

AI that handles real phone calls — inbound customer service, outbound reminders, appointment booking, lead qualification. Sub-400ms latency. 24/7. Any language.

Voice AgentsLast verified: April 2026

Voice agents vs voice tools: Voice tools (ElevenLabs, Whisper, Murf) generate or transcribe audio. Voice agents have conversations on the phone — they listen, respond, take actions, and complete tasks. Different category, different use cases.
