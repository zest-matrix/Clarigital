Fathom — The Complete Guide

Fathom records, transcribes and summarises meetings, then syncs the output to your CRM. Its notable feature is a genuinely usable free tier.

SimpleStart here

What it does

Fathom joins your calls, records and transcribes them, and produces a structured summary with action items.

You can highlight moments during the call with one click, and it generates clips and summaries around them.

Why sales teams use it

The CRM integration is the point. Call summaries, transcripts and next steps write into Salesforce or HubSpot automatically.

Manual CRM logging after calls is universally disliked and universally incomplete. Automating it improves data quality more than any process change.

The free tier

Unlike most competitors, Fathom's free tier covers unlimited recording and transcription for individuals.

That makes it an easy trial and a reasonable permanent choice for people who do not need team features.

WorkingBuild it

Summaries and templates

Fathom produces different summary formats by meeting type — sales discovery, customer success, interviews, general.

Choosing the right template matters more than most people expect; a sales template surfaces objections and next steps that a general summary omits.

Live highlighting

Marking a moment during the call is a small feature with disproportionate value. It tells the tool what mattered rather than leaving it to infer.

It also produces a shareable clip, which is more persuasive internally than a quoted line from a transcript.

Practical limits

It joins as a visible bot, which is unsuitable for some sensitive or client-facing contexts.

Transcription accuracy degrades with crosstalk and strong accents, as with all such tools. Verify quotes before relying on them.

DeepGo deeper

Against Granola, Otter and Fireflies

Granola takes a different approach — no bot, enhances your own notes, better for personal note-taking. Otter is strongest on pure transcription. Fireflies has the deepest workflow automation.

Fathom sits between them with the best free tier and straightforward CRM sync. Choose on whether your priority is personal notes, complete records or CRM data.

Consent and recording

Recording consent requirements vary by jurisdiction and several require all-party consent.

A visible bot provides implicit notice, which is one argument in its favour. It does not substitute for explicit consent where the law requires it.
