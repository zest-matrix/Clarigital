Otter.ai for business meetings

This guide focuses on Otter.ai's meeting-specific capabilities — OtterPilot, AI meeting summaries, action item tracking, and team collaboration features — for business teams, sales professionals and managers. For a full overview of Otter's transcription capabilities including lecture recording, interview transcription and audio file uploads, see the Otter.ai Voice & Audio guide.

OtterPilot is Otter's AI meeting agent. It joins any scheduled Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams call automatically, transcribes in real time, identifies speakers, tracks action items as they are assigned, and delivers a structured summary and action item list within minutes of the meeting ending. On Business plans, OtterPilot can attend up to 3 concurrent meetings — useful for managers whose team members are in simultaneous calls.

The meeting-specific value of Otter.ai is not just transcription but meeting accountability. Action items are extracted and attributed to specific people. The full transcript is searchable. Anyone who missed the meeting can catch up in 2 minutes rather than watching the full recording. Decisions are documented and findable months later.

Meeting-specific features

OtterPilot — Auto-joins all calendar-scheduled meetings. Starts recording when the meeting begins, stops when it ends. Sends transcript and summary to the host within minutes.

Real-time live captions — During the meeting, Otter displays live captions in a separate browser tab. Useful for accessibility and for catching up on what was just said without interrupting.

Action item tracking — Otter automatically identifies when an action is assigned during a meeting — "Can you send that by Thursday?" — and extracts it as a trackable action item attributed to the right person.

Meeting Gems — Highlight, comment on and tag specific parts of any transcript. Create shareable clips of key moments for team members who were not in the meeting.

Sales integration — On Business plans: Otter Sales Agent automatically pushes meeting notes to Salesforce and HubSpot and extracts sales-specific insights from customer calls.

Otter AI Chat — After meetings are recorded, ask natural language questions across your meeting history. Searches all transcripts to answer questions about what was discussed, decided or committed to.

Otter vs Fireflies for business meetings

Otter has better transcription accuracy for standard English and is simpler to set up. Fireflies has broader language support (100+ vs 3), stronger conversation intelligence, and more CRM integrations. For English-speaking business teams: Otter is sufficient and easier. For international teams or sales teams needing deep call analytics: Fireflies has more capability.

Getting started for business teams

Sign up at otter.ai. Connect your Google or Microsoft calendar (Settings → Integrations → Calendar). Enable OtterPilot to auto-join meetings. Set your notification preferences — Otter can send the summary to all meeting participants, just the host, or specific recipients. On Business plans: set up your shared workspace so all team members see relevant meeting transcripts.

12 Otter.ai meeting workflows

One-on-one meeting records
Enable OtterPilot for all calendar meetings. After each 1:1: review the action items Otter extracted, add any that were missed manually, send them to your direct report as the follow-up email. At your next 1:1: open the previous meeting's notes to review what was committed. Otter becomes your meeting memory across all direct reports.
Sales discovery call capture
For a prospect discovery call: OtterPilot transcribes the full conversation. After the call: review the AI summary for pain points mentioned, budget signals, timeline, decision makers. Copy relevant points to your CRM manually (Pro) or use Otter Sales Agent for automatic Salesforce/HubSpot push (Business). The transcript is your full reference for proposal writing.
Team standup notes
For daily or weekly standups: OtterPilot records the 15-minute call. After: the summary shows what each team member reported. Share the transcript with any absent team members as an instant catch-up. Over time, the standup archive becomes a project progress record — searchable by person, project or date.
Board or exec meeting documentation
For high-stakes meetings where complete accuracy matters: OtterPilot transcribes the full session. After: review the transcript carefully, correct any misidentified speakers, verify key decisions are correctly captured. Share the verified transcript with all attendees as the official meeting record. Better than handwritten minutes for speed and completeness.
Client meeting follow-up
After a client meeting: open the Otter transcript. Copy the key action items into your follow-up email. Quote specific things the client said to demonstrate you listened: 'As you mentioned, your current process requires three manual approvals for each invoice...' This level of specificity in follow-up emails differentiates you from competitors who send generic recaps.
Concurrent meeting coverage
On Business plan with OtterPilot attending up to 3 concurrent meetings: a manager can cover parallel team meetings happening simultaneously. After: review the summaries of all 3 meetings to stay informed across workstreams without attending each in full. Flag anything that needs follow-up from any of the 3 sessions.
Search across meeting history
Someone references a decision made 3 months ago. Open Otter AI Chat. Ask: 'What did we decide about [topic] in the [month/quarter]?' Otter searches all transcripts and returns the relevant excerpt with a link to the exact timestamp in the recording. This pays for itself the first time you find a disputed decision in seconds rather than hours.
Meeting clip for context sharing
In any transcript: highlight a section where an important decision was made or a key point was discussed. Create a shareable clip. Send the clip link to a colleague who needs that specific context. They see a 2-minute video excerpt with the transcript — exactly the context they need without having to watch the full recording.
Onboarding new team members
New team members need context on ongoing projects and decisions. Share access to your meeting archive in Otter workspace. New hire can search for any project name, client or topic and read through relevant meeting summaries. Context from months of meetings is accessible in minutes — faster than scheduling knowledge transfer calls.
Interview panel coordination
After each interview: Otter transcript is shared with the full hiring panel before the debrief meeting. Each panelist reads the transcript independently, adds written comments and ratings in Otter Meeting Gems. The debrief meeting starts with everyone informed — actual discussion rather than everyone reporting what they remember.

Tips

The 30-minute free plan cap kills momentum. The most common failure mode for Otter adoption is using the free plan, having the transcription stop mid-meeting, and losing trust in the tool. If you are evaluating Otter for real business use, test on the Pro plan ($8.33/month annual) from the start. The free plan is for testing basic functionality only.

Announce recording at the start of every meeting. OtterPilot joining as a visible bot is good — it signals to participants that the meeting is being recorded and provides consent. Still, make it a habit to verbally confirm at the start: "I'm using Otter to take notes today" — this keeps everyone informed and comfortable.

Technical background

See the main Otter.ai guide in the Voice & Audio section for full technical detail. Meeting-specific notes: OtterPilot uses calendar integration to auto-join scheduled meetings. Speaker identification uses voice profiling that improves over time as the system learns your team's voices. On Business plans, Otter Sales Agent uses AI to extract sales-specific insights (pain points, objections, buying signals) and push them to connected CRMs via API integration.

  • Business: $19.99/user/month — 6,000 min/month, 4-hour cap, 3 concurrent OtterPilot meetings, Salesforce/HubSpot, team admin
  • Enterprise: Custom — SSO, HIPAA, SOC 2, API
