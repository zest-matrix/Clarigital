What is Beautiful.ai?

Beautiful.ai is an AI-powered presentation maker built around one central idea: no matter what content you add, the slides always look professionally designed. Its patented Smart Slides engine reformats your slide in real time as you type — adjusting spacing, alignment, font sizes and visual hierarchy automatically. You cannot accidentally make a slide that looks broken.

This is a fundamentally different approach from Gamma (which generates full decks from prompts) or PowerPoint (which gives you blank canvases). Beautiful.ai sits in between: you control the content and narrative, the AI controls the design. DesignerBot generates first drafts from prompts, but the platform's real strength is the editing experience — making dozens of slides look consistently professional with minimal manual formatting.

Over 100,000 businesses across 193 countries have created over 100 million slides on Beautiful.ai. The company raised $45 million from General Catalyst in March 2026. It is powered by Anthropic's Claude for its AI content generation features, per Beautiful.ai's official website.

Smart Slides — how it works

Every Beautiful.ai slide uses a Smart layout. As you add bullet points, the layout adjusts. Add a fourth item to a three-column layout and it rebalances. Increase font size and surrounding elements rescale. Delete content and whitespace redistributes. The result is that any edit maintains visual balance without manual intervention. For teams producing high volumes of decks — sales teams, consultants, marketing departments — this eliminates the formatting rework that consumes a significant portion of presentation time.

The 2026 update added outline-first generation: DesignerBot now produces a text outline for review before committing to slide design. You can rewrite, reorder and restructure the narrative before the AI applies layouts. This addresses the main complaint about AI presentation tools — losing narrative control when the AI designs first.

Who Beautiful.ai is for

Teams and individuals who produce presentations regularly and prioritise design consistency. Particularly valuable for: sales teams needing consistent branded decks, consultancies producing client deliverables, marketing teams running multiple campaigns, managers who make a lot of internal decks and hate formatting. No free plan — 14-day trial with full access. Pro: $12/month (annual).

Getting started

Go to beautiful.ai. Start a 14-day free trial (credit card required). Create a presentation: choose New from DesignerBot (AI-generated) or New from Template. If using DesignerBot: type your prompt, review the generated outline, edit the structure, then click Generate. Beautiful.ai applies Smart Slide layouts to your content. Apply your brand theme — upload logo, set colours and fonts once under Brand settings. All future decks use these automatically.

12 Beautiful.ai use cases

Sales proposal deck
Use DesignerBot: 'Create a sales proposal for [prospect name] at [company]. We are proposing [solution]. Include: Understanding of their challenge, Our solution, Key benefits (3), How we work together, Timeline, Investment, Next steps.' After generation: review the outline, edit the narrative, then let Smart Slides design it. Apply your brand theme. The result is a professional branded proposal in under 15 minutes.
Team training deck
Create a training presentation on [topic] for [team]. Include: Why this matters, Core concepts (3–4), How to apply it, Common mistakes to avoid, Key resources. Beautiful.ai's Smart Slides ensure every section looks balanced even as you add or remove content during the iterative editing process that training content typically requires.
Executive status update
Monthly status deck structure: 1) Headline metric and status vs target, 2) Wins this period, 3) Issues and risks, 4) Key decisions needed, 5) Next period priorities. Beautiful.ai's table and chart Smart Slides handle data-heavy slides without manual alignment. Duplicate this template monthly and update the content.
Conference presentation
Use DesignerBot with the outline: '1) The problem we set out to solve, 2) Our approach and methodology, 3) Key findings (with data), 4) Implications and recommendations, 5) Q&A.' Beautiful.ai's image generation adds relevant visuals to each section. The design output meets conference professional standards without a designer.
Brand template setup
Before creating any decks: go to Brand → upload your logo, set primary and secondary brand colours, set heading and body fonts. Lock your logo placement across all slides. Set a default theme that applies your brand automatically to every new presentation. This one-time setup means every deck your team creates is automatically on-brand.
Investor update deck
Quarterly investor update structure: Portfolio highlights, Revenue metrics, Key challenges, Product roadmap progress, Team updates, Next quarter focus. Beautiful.ai's chart Smart Slides handle revenue graphs and KPI dashboards with clean, professional formatting that meets investor expectations.
Onboarding deck for new clients
Create a client onboarding presentation: Welcome and team introductions, How our process works (3–5 steps), What we need from you, Timeline and milestones, Communication and escalation, Next steps. Lock your logo and brand colours in the template so every client gets a consistent onboarding experience.
Content library for sales team
Create a shared slide library: product features, case studies, pricing tables, competitor comparisons, testimonials. Lock designs so sales reps cannot break the layout. They select the slides they need for each prospect and assemble custom decks from approved content. Consistent brand, flexible assembly.
DesignerBot outline editing
After generating an outline: reorder sections by dragging, delete any slide that does not fit your narrative, add a slide the AI missed by typing it in, edit bullet points to match your actual data and messaging. Only then click Generate. This two-step workflow gives you narrative control while letting the AI handle all visual design.
PowerPoint compatibility export
After completing your Beautiful.ai deck: File → Export → PowerPoint. Share the PPTX with clients, partners or colleagues who work in PowerPoint. Note: some Smart Slide elements export as static images rather than editable shapes. For presentations you plan to hand off for further editing in PowerPoint, design with simpler layouts.

Tips

Invest 10 minutes in Brand setup before your first real deck. Setting your logo, colours and fonts once means every deck you create afterwards is automatically on brand. This is the single highest-return action in Beautiful.ai.

Use the outline step to control the story. The 2026 outline-first workflow is the most important improvement. Do not skip it — restructure the narrative at the outline stage before letting the AI design. Changing the story after Smart Slides has been applied is harder than changing the outline.

Technical background

Beautiful.ai was founded in 2015 by Mitch Grasso (previously founder of SlideRocket, acquired by VMware in 2011) and is headquartered in San Francisco. The company raised $45 million from General Catalyst in March 2026, bringing total funding over $61 million, per public funding announcements. Over 100,000 businesses across 193 countries have used the platform, per the official Beautiful.ai website. AI content generation is powered by Anthropic's Claude, per Beautiful.ai's official product page.

Pricing (verified April 2026)

  • Pro: $12/month (annual) or $45/month — unlimited slides, Smart Slides, DesignerBot, PDF/PPT export, analytics
  • Team: $40/user/month (annual) — brand control, shared libraries, collaboration, admin tools
  • Enterprise: Custom — SSO, user provisioning, priority support, dedicated account management
