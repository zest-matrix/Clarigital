What is Gamma?

Gamma is an AI presentation, document and website builder. Type a prompt — "Create a pitch deck for a sustainable fashion startup" — and Gamma generates a complete, structured presentation with text, images and professional layouts in under 60 seconds. Over 70 million users use Gamma, and it has reached $100M in annual recurring revenue at a $2.1B valuation, making it the most widely adopted AI presentation tool available.

Gamma uses a card-based, scrollable format rather than traditional slides. Each "card" is a self-contained section that can contain text, images, charts, embedded videos, interactive forms, or live web content. Cards can be exported as PowerPoint, PDF, Google Slides or shared as a live web link. The web-native format also means presentations can function as standalone websites — useful for landing pages, proposals or product showcases that need to live online rather than in an email attachment.

The Gamma Agent (introduced in Gamma 3.0) goes further than one-shot generation: it researches the web, refines content iteratively, suggests design improvements, and responds to natural language editing instructions across the entire deck. "Make this more concise", "Switch to a dark theme", "Add a slide comparing our pricing to competitors" — all handled conversationally.

What Gamma produces

Presentations — The core use case. AI generates full decks from a prompt, outline or uploaded document (PDF, PPTX, Word, URL). Export to PowerPoint, PDF or Google Slides, or share via web link.

Documents — Long-form content in Gamma's visual format. Product specs, reports, proposals — formatted automatically with images, callouts and visual hierarchy.

Websites — One-click publish any Gamma deck as a website. Useful for portfolios, landing pages, event pages and client-facing proposals where a permanent URL beats an attachment.

Social posts — Convert any Gamma content into social media format cards for LinkedIn or other platforms.

Who Gamma is for

Founders building pitch decks. Consultants producing client proposals. Marketers making campaign decks. Educators creating course content. Anyone who needs to turn structured information into a polished visual presentation quickly. The free tier (400 AI credits) is genuinely useful for evaluation. Plus ($8/month annual) removes the watermark and unlocks professional use.

Gamma vs PowerPoint

PowerPoint gives you maximum control. Gamma gives you maximum speed. For most business presentations where content matters more than pixel-perfect control, Gamma produces professional results in minutes that would take hours in PowerPoint. For presentations requiring precise custom layouts, complex animations or brand template compliance, PowerPoint or Beautiful.ai is better suited.

Getting started

Go to gamma.app. Create a free account (no credit card required). Click Create → Generate with AI. Enter your prompt or paste an outline. Choose your theme and number of cards. Click Generate. Gamma produces the deck in under 60 seconds. Edit any card by clicking it — text, images, layout all editable. Share via link or export.

12 Gamma prompts

Pitch deck from scratch
Create a 10-slide pitch deck for [company name], a [describe the business in one sentence]. Include slides for: Problem, Solution, Market size, Business model, Traction (mention [key metrics if any]), Team, and Ask. Tone: confident, data-driven. Audience: early-stage investors. Use a [clean/bold/minimal] visual theme.
Import and transform a document
Upload a Word doc, PDF or paste a URL. Prompt Gamma Agent: 'Convert this document into a presentation. Keep the key headings as slide titles. Summarise each section to 3 bullet points maximum. Add a visual on each slide relevant to the content. Make it suitable for a 10-minute executive briefing.'
Quarterly business review deck
Create a QBR presentation for [team/department] covering Q[N] [year]. Include: Executive summary, Key results vs targets, What worked and why, Challenges and learnings, Q[N+1] priorities, Resources needed. Data: [paste your key numbers]. Keep each slide to one key message.
Client proposal
Create a consulting proposal for [client name] covering [project description]. Include: Understanding of the problem, Proposed approach, Deliverables and timeline, Team and credentials, Investment (placeholder pricing table). Tone: professional, confident. Format for web sharing as a live link rather than attachment.
Product launch deck
Create a product launch presentation for [product name]. Slides: What it is (one sentence), The problem it solves, How it works (3 steps), Key features (3), Target customer, Pricing, Launch timeline, Call to action. Audience: internal sales team. Keep it punchy — this is a sales enablement deck, not a technical spec.
Training or onboarding content
Create a new employee onboarding presentation for [company name] covering: Company mission and values, How we work (key processes), Tools we use, Team structure, First 30-day priorities, Who to contact for what. Tone: welcoming and clear. Publish as a website link to share with all new hires.
Website from a presentation
Take this existing Gamma deck and publish it as a website. Prompt: 'Optimise this for web reading. Add a clear hero section at the top, make the navigation anchor-linked, and ensure each section has a clear headline visible when scrolling. This will live at [your domain] as a product landing page.'
Competitive analysis
Create a competitive analysis presentation comparing [your product] against [Competitor A], [Competitor B] and [Competitor C]. Include: Feature comparison table, Pricing comparison, Target customer differences, Our key differentiators. Be factual and honest — this is for internal use, not marketing.
Gamma Agent for iterative editing
After generating a deck: open Gamma Agent (chat panel). Instructions: 'This deck is for [specific audience]. Make the opening card more compelling — it needs to hook a busy executive in 5 seconds. Also: the pricing slide feels apologetic — rewrite it to be more confident. Finally, add a FAQ card at the end addressing common objections.'
Repurpose to social cards
After creating a Gamma deck: prompt Agent: 'Convert the 5 most important insights from this deck into individual LinkedIn post cards. Each card: one key insight as a headline, 2-3 supporting points as bullets, and a brief conclusion. Format for maximum readability on mobile.'

Tips

Start with an outline, not a prompt. Gamma generates better decks when you give it structure. Spend 2 minutes writing your slide titles as bullet points before generating. Paste that outline as your prompt. The AI fills in the content under your structure rather than deciding the structure itself.

Free plan watermark blocks professional use. The free tier's "Made with Gamma" badge is visible to anyone who sees your deck. If this is for client work or investor meetings, upgrade to Plus ($8/month) before sharing. The watermark is the only meaningful limitation of the free tier for occasional professional use.

Technical background

Gamma was founded in 2020. Per Gamma's official website, it has over 70 million users and $100M ARR. The Generate API became generally available in January 2026, enabling programmatic deck creation. Gamma uses over 20 AI models for text and image generation, per official product documentation. The Nano Banana Pro model produces cinematic HD imagery within decks.

Pricing (verified April 2026)

  • Free: 400 AI credits (one-time, ~10–15 decks), Gamma branding on all output
  • Plus: $8/month (annual) or $10/month — unlimited AI (fair use), no branding, PowerPoint export, custom brand colours
  • Pro: $15/month (annual) or $20/month — premium AI models, analytics, API access, 10 custom domains
  • Ultra: Introductory pricing — most advanced models, 100 custom domains, early access features
