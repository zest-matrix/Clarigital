Apple Silicon: The Complete Guide to Apple’s AI Chips

Apple designs the chips inside every iPhone, iPad and Mac, and each one has AI hardware built in. What the Neural Engine does, how the M-series and A-series compare, who builds them, the servers behind Apple Intelligence, and the facts buried in Apple's filings. Three reading levels, every number sourced.

1.At a glance

SimpleStart here

Apple designs its own chips, called Apple silicon: the A-series for iPhone and the M-series for Mac and iPad 1 2. Every one since 2017 has a Neural Engine, a part of the chip built only for AI, and since late 2025 each graphics core also has its own AI unit, which Apple calls a Neural Accelerator 3 4. Apple does not sell its chips to other companies; they go into Apple products and Apple's own servers 5.

What it isChips with AI hardware built in, for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple's AI servers 6 5
Newest chipsM6 and A20 Pro, Apple's first 2 nm chips; M5 Ultra for Mac Studio 6 2
Who builds itApple designs; TSMC manufactures, including at its Arizona plant 7
Can you buy one?Only inside an Apple device; a Mac mini with M6 starts at $899 8
DeeperThe detail

Apple's approach differs from NVIDIA's: instead of one huge data-centre chip, it puts AI hardware into hundreds of millions of personal devices and runs AI on the device itself and on its own servers 9. Harder requests go to Private Cloud Compute, Apple's own servers running Apple silicon 5. Since June 2026 some requests also run on Google Cloud using NVIDIA GPUs, the first time Private Cloud Compute has run outside Apple's data centres 10.

ExpertFor specialists

Apple's June 2026 quarter: revenue $109.4 billion, up 16%; gross margin 50.1% 11 12. Research and development spending reached $34.0 billion in the first nine months of fiscal 2026, up about 33%, while spending on property and equipment fell to $6.8 billion from $9.5 billion 12. Apple sells no data-centre chip and discloses no Neural Engine performance figure for its chips after M4 13 6.

3.The story

SimpleStart here
  • 2008 Johny Srouji joins to lead the A4, the first Apple-designed system-on-a-chip 16.
  • 2017 A11 Bionic in iPhone X brings the first Neural Engine, for Face ID 3.
  • 2020 M1 moves the Mac to Apple silicon 1.
  • 2024 M4 launches; Apple Intelligence arrives on iPhone, iPad and Mac 13 19.
  • 2025 M5 puts an AI unit in every graphics core; Houston-built AI servers start shipping 4 20.
  • 2026 Google Gemini deal, M5 Ultra, and the first 2 nm chips, M6 and A20 Pro 21 6 2.
DeeperThe detail

The first Neural Engine managed 600 billion operations per second 3; M1's did 11 trillion 1 and M4's 38 trillion, which Apple called "60x faster than the first Neural Engine" 13. From 2025 Apple stopped quoting a single figure and instead compares each chip with the one before: M5 has "over 4x the peak GPU compute performance for AI" of M4 4.

ExpertFor specialists

Apple announced Private Cloud Compute in June 2024 as "custom-built server hardware that brings the power and security of Apple silicon to the data center" 5. In January 2026 Apple and Google said "the next generation of Apple Foundation Models will be based on Google's Gemini models and cloud technology", while Apple Intelligence "will continue to run on Apple devices and Private Cloud Compute" 21.

DeeperThe detail

Bigger M-series chips are built by joining smaller ones. M5 Pro and M5 Max join two dies into one chip, which Apple calls its "Fusion Architecture" 22. M5 Ultra joins two M5 Max chips, making it Apple's first four-die chip and the first Ultra with an AI unit in every graphics core 6.

ExpertFor specialists

Apple's AI features need a recent chip: at WWDC 2026 the next Apple Intelligence was listed for iPhone 15 Pro and later, iPad and Mac with M1 or later, MacBook Neo (A18 Pro), Vision Pro and recent Apple Watch models 24. Apple's announcements do not name the chip inside its servers; 9to5Mac, reading Apple's own server software release, reports that Apple has started using M5 chips (press report) 25.

5.How it’s made

SimpleStart here

Apple designs its chips and buys the manufacturing from TSMC 7.

  1. Design: Apple's hardware technologies team under Johny Srouji 16.
  2. Chip factory: TSMC. Apple expects to buy "well over 100 million advanced chips" from TSMC's Arizona plant in 2026 7.
  3. Process: 3 nm for M5 Pro and M5 Max; 2 nm for M6 and A20 Pro 22 6 2. Press names TSMC's N2 process for M6 26.
  4. Packaging: Amkor is building a $7 billion packaging plant in Peoria, Arizona, with Apple as its first and largest customer 7.
  5. Servers: assembled in Houston, Texas, with logic boards made on site 7.
DeeperThe detail

Apple says more than 20 billion chips have been made for it in the US, from 24 factories in 12 states 7. Its American Manufacturing Program, part of a $600 billion US pledge, includes TSMC, Amkor, Broadcom, Samsung, GlobalFoundries, Texas Instruments, Applied Materials, Corning, Coherent and GlobalWafers 27.

ExpertFor specialists

Apple's releases for M6 and A20 Pro say "2-nanometer" but do not name the factory 6 2. Apple's 10-K warns that new products "often utilize custom components available from only one source" and that most manufacturing is done by partners "located primarily in China mainland, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan and Vietnam" 14.

TSMC's Arizona site started high-volume 4 nm production in late 2024; its second plant targets 3 nm volume in the second half of 2027, and a third plant aims at 2 nm and A16 by the end of the decade 28. Apple does not say which of its chips come from Arizona rather than Taiwan 7.

ExpertFor specialists

In March 2026 Apple added TDK, Bosch, Cirrus Logic and Qnity Electronics to its US programme, with chips for Face ID to be made at GlobalFoundries in Malta, New York 30. The Amkor Peoria campus is planned at more than 750,000 square feet of cleanroom and up to 3,000 jobs, with Apple and NVIDIA named as customers 29.

7.How it works

SimpleStart here

Apple chips use "unified memory": the main processor, the graphics and the Neural Engine all share one pool of fast memory inside the chip package, so data does not have to be copied between them 1. That matters for AI because a model's size is limited by how much memory it can reach. The biggest Mac chip, M3 Ultra, could run language models "with over 600 billion parameters directly on device" 31.

DeeperThe detail

There are now two kinds of AI hardware on each chip. The Neural Engine runs everyday AI efficiently; since M5 and A19 Pro, a Neural Accelerator in every graphics core handles heavier matrix maths 4 23. M6 and A20 Pro double the Neural Engine, with two 16-core engines on one chip 6 2.

ExpertFor specialists

Apple's own software is MLX, an open-source framework that keeps arrays in shared memory 32. Its third-generation models include a 3-billion-parameter on-device model and a 20-billion-parameter sparse model that keeps the full model in flash and loads parts into memory as needed 9. Private Cloud Compute servers run a hardened version of iOS and macOS, keep no user data after a request, and publish their software images for outside checking 5.

10.The software lock-in

SimpleStart here

Apple controls the whole stack: the chip, the device, the operating system and the AI models 9. Because every part of the chip shares one pool of memory, a single Mac can hold a very large AI model: Apple says M3 Ultra can run models with over 600 billion parameters 31.

DeeperThe detail

Scale is the other advantage. Apple's AI features run on every recent iPhone, iPad and Mac, so Apple can run most AI on the device rather than paying for data-centre chips for every request 24 9. Its privacy design, Private Cloud Compute, publishes server software for outside researchers to inspect 5.

ExpertFor specialists

Apple has used its chips to argue for local AI: Mac Studio systems can be clustered, four at a time, for up to 3x faster AI inference, Apple says 33, and Mac mini with M6 claims up to 13.5x faster prompt processing than M1 in LM Studio 8.

11.Who buys it and why

SimpleStart here

Apple is the only user of Apple silicon. The chips go into Apple's own products and its Private Cloud Compute servers 5. Mac mini and Mac Studio are also popular with developers who want to run AI models on their desks 33.

DeeperThe detail

Apple does not use its own chips for everything. Its 2024 models were trained on Google TPUs: 8,192 TPUv4 chips for the server model and 2,048 TPUv5p for the on-device model 39. Its 2026 models were trained on Google's "latest generation of cloud TPU accelerators" 9.

ExpertFor specialists

The largest 2026 model, AFM 3 Cloud Pro, runs on NVIDIA GPUs in Google Cloud 9; that part of Private Cloud Compute uses NVIDIA Confidential Computing, Intel processors and Google's Titan security chip 10. Apple says its own Apple silicon servers still handle "the most demanding Apple Intelligence features" 10.

13.Weak spots

SimpleStart here

Limits, from Apple's own statements and reporting:

  • Late on AI: in March 2025 Apple said the personal Siri features would "take us longer than we thought" 41.
  • Relies on Google for its next-generation models and training chips 21 9.
  • No data-centre chip for sale: Apple sells chips only inside its own products 14.
  • Leadership change: a new CEO and a new AI chief in 2026 15 17.
DeeperThe detail

Apple's total capital spending, $12.7 billion in fiscal 2025 18, is small next to Meta's guidance of $130 to $145 billion for 2026 42. A dedicated AI server chip, reportedly code-named Baltra and developed with Broadcom, is press-reported for 2027; Apple has not confirmed it 43.

ExpertFor specialists

Memory costs cut into margins: Apple guided a 47–48% gross margin for the September 2026 quarter, down from 50.1%, and both figures include some benefit from tariff refunds 37 11. Apple described AI spending as "quite a bit more" across operating costs and cost of sales 37.

14.India angle

SimpleStart here

Apple has six stores in India, including Mumbai's BKC and Borivali, Delhi's Saket, Bengaluru, Pune and Noida 44. Apple Intelligence has worked in Indian English since March 2025 45. No Apple chip is made in India.

DeeperThe detail

India is now a major iPhone base. In May 2025 Tim Cook said the majority of iPhones sold in the US would have India as their country of origin 46. Press reports put iPhone exports at about ₹2 trillion from April 2025 to February 2026 47. Apple's 10-K lists India among its main manufacturing countries 14.

ExpertFor specialists

Press reports name Tata Electronics in Hosur, Tamil Nadu, and Foxconn in Devanahalli, Karnataka, as the main iPhone plants; Apple does not publish the list 48. For India's own chip plants, see our India's semiconductor push guide (coming soon).

15.What’s next

SimpleStart here

Only what Apple and its partners have announced:

  • Mac Studio with 512 GB: late October 2026 33.
  • Mac mini made in Houston: later in 2026 7.
  • Amkor Peoria packaging: production early 2028 29.
  • TSMC Arizona 3 nm: volume in the second half of 2027 28.
DeeperThe detail

The next Apple Foundation Models are based on Google's Gemini models 21; Apple says its third generation was "custom-built in collaboration with Google" 9. Apple has not announced a data-centre chip; press reports of a 2027 server chip are unconfirmed 43.

ExpertFor specialists

The question is whether Apple keeps AI mainly on devices, or builds data-centre capacity like its rivals. Its spending pattern so far, rising research and falling capital spending, points to the device 12.
