Amazon Trainium: The Complete Guide

Amazon's own AI chips, designed by its Annapurna Labs team and rented through AWS. What Trainium and Inferentia are, how they work, the prices AWS publishes, the gigawatt deals with Anthropic and OpenAI, and the facts buried in Amazon's filings. Three reading levels, every number sourced.

1.At a glance

SimpleStart here

Trainium is Amazon's own chip for training AI models, and Inferentia its chip for running them 1 2. Amazon does not sell the chips; it rents them through its cloud, AWS 3. They are designed by Annapurna Labs, a chip company Amazon bought in 2015 4 5. The newest chip, Trainium3, went on sale in December 2025 6.

What it isAmazon-designed chips for AI: Trainium (training and inference) and Inferentia (inference) 1 2
Latest chipTrainium3, on a 3 nm process, in Trn3 UltraServers 6
Who designs itAnnapurna Labs, part of AWS 5
Biggest usersAnthropic, OpenAI, and Amazon's own Bedrock service 7 8
DeeperThe detail

Amazon's chips have become a large business. In July 2026 Andy Jassy said Amazon's chips business had passed an annual revenue run rate of more than $25 billion 7; that figure covers Trainium, Graviton processors and Nitro chips together 3. He said Trainium2 is about 30% better on price-performance than comparable GPUs and "has largely sold out" 3.

ExpertFor specialists

Amazon's June 2026 quarter: net sales $200.6 billion (up 20%), AWS sales $42.2 billion (up 37%, its fastest growth in 18 quarters) and AWS operating income $16.6 billion 7. Trainium commitments stood at more than $225 billion in April 2026 9. Amazon planned about $200 billion of capital spending for 2026 in February 8; on the July call it raised that to about $220 billion, citing higher memory costs 10.

DeeperThe detail

The chips come from Annapurna Labs, an Israeli start-up Amazon bought in 2015 4. It designs AWS's Nitro, Graviton and AI chips, from a lab on the outskirts of Austin, Texas 5; Amazon's jobs site also lists Annapurna teams in Haifa and Tel Aviv, Israel, and Boston 14. The purchase price was not disclosed by Amazon; press reports put it at about $350 million 15.

ExpertFor specialists

3.The story

SimpleStart here
  • 2015 Amazon buys Annapurna Labs 4.
  • 2018 Announces Inferentia, its first AI chip 17.
  • 2020 Announces Trainium 18.
  • 2022 Trainium instances (Trn1) go on sale 19.
  • 2023 Inferentia2 on sale; Trainium2 announced 20 21.
  • 2024 Trainium2 on sale 22.
  • 2025 Project Rainier for Anthropic switches on; Trainium3 goes on sale 23 6.
  • 2026 OpenAI deal for about 2 gigawatts of Trainium; Anthropic deal grows to up to 5 gigawatts 24 25.
DeeperThe detail

Amazon started with inference. Inferentia was announced in November 2018 17 and its Inf1 instances launched in December 2019 with up to 16 chips 26. Trn1, the first Trainium instances, went on sale in October 2022 with up to 50% lower training cost than comparable GPU instances, on AWS's claim 19. Trainium2 was announced in November 2023 21 and went on sale in December 2024, when Trainium3 was also first announced 22.

ExpertFor specialists

Project Rainier, the Trainium2 cluster AWS built for Anthropic, was activated in October 2025 with nearly 500,000 chips, "70% larger than any prior AWS AI platform"; Amazon said Anthropic would have more than a million Trainium2 chips by the end of 2025 23. By February 2026 AWS had landed 1.4 million Trainium2 chips, all subscribed 8.

DeeperThe detail

Despite the name, Trainium now does most of the inference too: Amazon says Trainium2 powers the majority of inference on Amazon Bedrock, its service for running AI models 8. Jassy wrote in April 2026 that Trainium3, which "just started shipping at the start of 2026", is 30 to 40% more price-performant than Trainium2 and "nearly fully-subscribed" 3.

ExpertFor specialists

Trainium4 is promised to deliver at least six times Trainium3's FP4 performance, three times its FP8 performance and four times its memory bandwidth 27. In April 2026 Jassy said it was "still about 18 months from broad availability", with a significant share already reserved 3. AWS has not published its process node or memory size 27.

5.How it’s made

SimpleStart here

Amazon designs its chips, but does not say who manufactures them 5.

  1. Design: Annapurna Labs, in Austin, Texas and Israel 5 14.
  2. Chip factory: not disclosed by Amazon. Press reports name TSMC for Trainium2 and Trainium3 28 15.
  3. Memory: Trainium3 carries 144 GB of HBM3e memory 6; Amazon does not name the supplier.
  4. Servers: AWS builds Trn3 UltraServers of up to 144 chips, joined by its own NeuronSwitch fabric 6 27.
DeeperThe detail

Trainium3 is "the first 3nm AI chip from AWS" 27. Other partners are named only in the press: CommonWealth Magazine calls Taiwan's Alchip Trainium's main design-services provider and says servers are assembled mainly by Wiwynn 29. Marvell has an official five-year agreement to supply AWS with "custom AI products" and networking chips, but its release does not name Trainium 30.

ExpertFor specialists

The Austin lab itself is small: press who toured it in March 2026 described it as about the size of two large conference rooms, with a separate test data centre in a co-location facility, and said Trainium3 is liquid-cooled 15. AWS has not published street addresses for its chip labs 5.

7.How it works

SimpleStart here

Each Trainium chip has several NeuronCores. Inside each core, a large grid of maths circuits (a 128 by 128 "systolic array") does the matrix multiplication that dominates AI, while smaller engines handle everything else 36. Chips link to each other with AWS's NeuronLink, and servers join through AWS's EFA network 23.

DeeperThe detail

A NeuronCore has four engines: a tensor engine for matrix maths, a vector engine for sums across data, a scalar engine for functions such as GELU, and a "GpSimd" engine of eight programmable processors for custom code 36. Each core has its own software-managed on-chip memory (24 MiB on the first Trainium) 36. Trainium2 has eight NeuronCores per chip and adds support for "structured sparsity", skipping zeros for up to four times the speed 37 38.

ExpertFor specialists

Trainium3's cores (NeuronCore-v4) run the new MXFP8 and MXFP4 number formats at four times their BF16 speed, speed up the exponential maths used in attention fourfold, and add hardware support for routing in mixture-of-experts models 39 1. Its UltraServer uses NeuronSwitch-v1, an all-to-all fabric with twice the bandwidth within each UltraServer 27. Software is the Neuron SDK: PyTorch, JAX, vLLM and Hugging Face work on it, and NKI lets engineers program the chip directly 1. Neuron 2.27 (December 2025) added native PyTorch ("TorchNeuron") and an open-source NKI compiler, both in private beta 40.

10.The software lock-in

SimpleStart here

Amazon's advantage is that it controls the chip, the servers, the network and the cloud where customers already run their businesses 5. Its biggest AI service, Bedrock, runs most of its inference on Trainium 3, so Amazon is its own largest customer.

DeeperThe detail

AWS's Neuron software is the answer to NVIDIA's CUDA: it supports PyTorch, JAX, vLLM, Hugging Face Transformers and TorchTitan, and NKI lets engineers write low-level code for the chip 1. Anthropic's engineers write kernels for Trainium together with Annapurna Labs and contribute to Neuron 46. The latest version, Neuron 2.32, supports every generation from Inferentia to Trainium3 47.

ExpertFor specialists

Amazon is also opening Trainium to NVIDIA's ecosystem: Trainium4 will support NVIDIA's NVLink Fusion connection 27. Jassy wrote that the chips business would be worth a run rate of about $50 billion if Amazon sold its chips at market prices to AWS and others, and that it is "quite possible" Amazon will sell racks to third parties 3.

11.Who buys it and why

SimpleStart here

Anthropic is the anchor customer. Amazon has invested $8 billion in it and AWS is its "primary training partner" 48. In April 2026 the deal grew: Anthropic committed more than $100 billion to AWS over ten years, for up to 5 gigawatts of capacity, and already uses more than a million Trainium2 chips 25.

DeeperThe detail
  • OpenAI (February 2026): Amazon agreed to invest $50 billion ($15 billion now, $35 billion on conditions); OpenAI will use about 2 gigawatts of Trainium3 and Trainium4 capacity, within an AWS agreement expanded by $100 billion over eight years 24.
  • Named Trn2 customers: Databricks, poolside, Itaú Unibanco, Ricoh, Hugging Face, Datadog and others 44.
  • Trainium3 customers: Anthropic, Karakuri, Metagenomi, NetoAI, Ricoh, Splash Music and Decart, several reporting up to 50% lower costs 27.
  • Apple: said at re:Invent 2024 it was "in the early stages of evaluating Trainium 2" 49.
  • Amazon itself: Rufus, its shopping assistant, used more than 80,000 Inferentia and Trainium chips on Prime Day 2024 50.
ExpertFor specialists

Amazon's July 2026 release named "multi-year, multi-gigawatt commitments" from Anthropic and OpenAI, plus Trainium customers including TwelveLabs, Poolside, Uber and Pinterest 7. Anthropic also uses Google's TPUs and NVIDIA's GPUs, and signed for up to a million Google TPUs in October 2025 51.

12.Hidden in plain sight

SimpleStart here

Public facts buried in Amazon's filings and letters. Each links to the exact document.

  1. Anthropic made Amazon $53.4 billion in one quarter, as pre-tax gains, mostly on its Anthropic stake, in the June 2026 quarter 7.
  2. The chips business is racing: Trainium and Graviton alone passed a $10 billion run rate in February 2026 8; by July Amazon said its chips business had passed $25 billion 7.
  3. Trainium commitments passed $225 billion by April 2026 9.
  4. Amazon's cash flow turned negative: free cash flow over the twelve months to June 2026 was an outflow of $7.6 billion, as capital spending rose 64% 7.
  5. Anthropic's spending is AWS revenue: the filing says the commercial deal is "primarily for the provision of AWS cloud services, which includes the use of AWS chips" 52.
DeeperThe detail
  1. Amazon's Anthropic notes were valued at $42.2 billion at 31 March 2026, against $8 billion invested 52.
  2. The new $20 billion Anthropic facility pays out only as compute milestones are reached 52.
  3. Few suppliers: "We rely on a limited group of suppliers for semiconductor products" 53.
  4. Trainium2 was sold out: 1.4 million chips landed and "fully subscribed" by February 2026 8.
  5. Most of Bedrock runs on Trainium, not NVIDIA 8.
ExpertFor specialists
  1. OpenAI's $35 billion second tranche is mandatory on milestones or an IPO before 31 December 2028 52.
  2. Total commitments reached $569.3 billion at 31 March 2026, including $106.3 billion of leases not yet started 52.
  3. Trainium3's BF16 speed per core did not rise over Trainium2; the gain is in 8-bit and 4-bit maths 38 39.
  4. Capital spending in the June quarter alone was $54.2 billion 7.
  5. AWS's water use is 0.15 litres per kilowatt-hour, against 0.375 for the industry, by Amazon's figures 23.

13.Weak spots

SimpleStart here

Limits, from Amazon's own documents and reporting:

  • Few suppliers for semiconductors 53.
  • Only through AWS: Trainium is not sold as a chip 3.
  • Two customers dominate: Anthropic and OpenAI hold the multi-gigawatt commitments 7.
  • Software still maturing: native PyTorch was only in private beta in December 2025 40.
DeeperThe detail

Customers have not all been happy. Business Insider reported an internal Amazon document from July 2025 in which Cohere found Trainium1 and Trainium2 underperforming NVIDIA's H100 and access to Trainium2 "extremely limited", and Stability AI called Trainium2 "less competitive" on latency and cost; Amazon said Trainium2 was fully subscribed 54. These are press reports.

ExpertFor specialists

Anthropic, the anchor customer, spreads its work across Trainium, Google TPUs and NVIDIA GPUs 51. AWS publishes no on-demand price for Trainium2 or Trainium3, which makes outside comparison harder 44 45. Amazon's annual report also names the availability and price of "land, water, and energy" as a risk 53.

14.India angle

SimpleStart here

In June 2026 Amazon announced $48 billion of investment in India from 2026 to 2030 55. AWS plans $21 billion for cloud and AI infrastructure in India from 2026 to 2030, including a new data centre in Bharat Future City, Hyderabad, announced in July 2026 56.

DeeperThe detail

AWS runs two Indian regions: Mumbai (since 2016) and Hyderabad (since November 2022) 57. Inferentia2 instances have been available in Mumbai since December 2023 31, and Trainium (Trn1) can be booked there by Capacity Block at $9.532 an hour 32. We found no official listing of Trainium2 or Trainium3 in India.

ExpertFor specialists

Amazon's 2023 India plan was ₹1,05,600 crore (US$12.7 billion) by 2030 57; the 2026 figures are much larger 55 56. Under the IndiaAI Mission, AWS offers its AI services through an empanelled partner, SHI India; the chip types were not specified 58.

15.What’s next

SimpleStart here

Only what Amazon has announced:

  • Trainium3: ramping through 2026; nearly all supply expected to be committed by mid-2026 8.
  • Trainium4: deliveries expected to begin in 2027 24.
  • Anthropic: nearly 1 gigawatt of Trainium2 and Trainium3 by the end of 2026 25.
DeeperThe detail

On the July 2026 call Jassy said there is "a real chance" Amazon will sell Trainium outside AWS in future 10. Anthropic plans Trainium3 capacity later in 2026 and more inference capacity in Asia and Europe 25.

ExpertFor specialists

The test is whether Trainium can win customers beyond its two anchors. OpenAI's 2 gigawatts depend on Trainium3 and Trainium4 arriving on time 24, and AWS has not yet said who makes Trainium4 or on what process 27.
