Meta MTIA: The Complete Guide

Meta's own AI chips, built to rank the posts and ads billions of people see, and now moving into training and generative AI. What MTIA is, how it works, who builds it, the Broadcom deal, and the facts buried in Meta's filings. Three reading levels, every number sourced.

1.At a glance

SimpleStart here

MTIA (Meta Training and Inference Accelerator) is Meta's own family of AI chips 1. They began as chips for the recommendation systems that decide which posts, reels and ads each person sees on Facebook and Instagram 1 2. Meta says it has "deployed hundreds of thousands of MTIA chips in production" 3. In March 2026 it announced four new generations, one roughly every six months 3.

What it isAI chips designed for Meta's own ranking, recommendation and, now, generative AI work 4
Latest chipMTIA 300, in production for training; MTIA 400 next 3
Who builds itCo-developed with Broadcom; TSMC made earlier generations 5 6
Can you buy one?No; Meta uses them only in its own data centres 4
DeeperThe detail

Meta runs several kinds of AI chip side by side: its own MTIA plus millions of NVIDIA GPUs and up to 6 gigawatts of AMD GPUs 7 8. On the April 2026 call Mark Zuckerberg said Meta is "rolling out more than 1GW of our own custom silicon that we're developing with Broadcom" 9. Meta's 2025 paper said its second-generation chip cut total cost of ownership by 44% compared with GPUs 2.

ExpertFor specialists

Meta's June 2026 quarter: revenue $60.8 billion, up 28%; capital spending $31.08 billion including finance-lease payments 10. It guides 2026 capital spending of $130 to $145 billion and total expenses of $165 to $169 billion 10. MTIA was not named on the call; CFO Susan Li called the "internal custom silicon effort" a source of "long-term strategic flexibility and supply chain leverage" 11.

3.The story

SimpleStart here
  • 2020 Meta starts the MTIA programme 1.
  • 2023 Reveals MTIA v1, now called MTIA 100, and its video chip, MSVP 1 16.
  • 2024 Next-generation MTIA, now called MTIA 200, in production in 16 regions 6.
  • 2025 Publishes a detailed paper on the chip 17.
  • 2026 Announces MTIA 300, 400, 450 and 500 in March, and a multi-gigawatt Broadcom deal in April 3 5.
DeeperThe detail

MTIA began as an inference chip for recommendation models, the systems that rank content and ads 1. Meta renamed the first two chips in 2026: v1 is now "MTIA 100" and the second generation (also called MTIA 2i) is "MTIA 200" 3. In January 2026 Susan Li said MTIA would add ranking and recommendation training from the first quarter of 2026 13, and MTIA 300 is now in production for that 3.

ExpertFor specialists

The road was not smooth. Reuters reported that Meta scrapped an in-house inference chip after a failed test in 2022 and then ordered billions of dollars of NVIDIA GPUs 18; in February 2026 The Information reported Meta had scrapped its most advanced training chip after design problems 19. Both are press reports. In October 2025 Meta bought Rivos, a RISC-V chip start-up; a Meta spokesperson said "this will further accelerate our efforts" 20.

DeeperThe detail

Meta says the 400, 450 and 500 share the "same chassis, rack, and network infrastructure", so each new chip drops into existing racks 3. From MTIA 300 to MTIA 500, Meta says memory bandwidth rises 4.5 times and compute 25 times, partly by moving to smaller number formats 3. It plans new chips "every six months or less" 4.

ExpertFor specialists

Meta's official step-ups: MTIA 400 has four times more FP8 compute than MTIA 300 (Meta says "400% higher") and 51% more memory bandwidth; MTIA 450 doubles MTIA 400's bandwidth and has 75% more 4-bit compute; MTIA 500 adds 50% bandwidth, up to 80% more memory and 43% more 4-bit compute 3. Reuters reported from an internal memo that a chip code-named "Iris" enters production in September 2026 21.

5.How it’s made

SimpleStart here

Meta designs MTIA with Broadcom's help. For the first two generations Meta named TSMC as the factory 1 6.

  1. Design: Meta, co-developed with Broadcom "across chip design, packaging, and networking" 5.
  2. Chip factory: TSMC 7 nm for MTIA 100 and 5 nm for MTIA 200 1 6. Meta has not named the factory for the 2026 chips; a Reuters report of an internal memo says TSMC 21.
  3. Memory: the first two generations used cheap LPDDR5 memory; from MTIA 300, stacked HBM3E (216 GB on MTIA 300) 2 22.
  4. Racks: up to 72 chips per rack, built to Open Compute Project designs 6 4.
DeeperThe detail

Broadcom says the partnership runs "through 2029", starts with more than 1 gigawatt, and includes "the industry's first 2nm AI compute accelerator" 23. Broadcom's CEO Hock Tan left Meta's board to become an adviser on its chip roadmap 5. Neither company disclosed a dollar value 5.

ExpertFor specialists

The newer chips are built from chiplets: MTIA 300 has a 3 nm compute die and a 5 nm input/output die, as presented at Hot Chips 2026 24, plus two network chiplets, each with six 800 Gbps network interfaces 22. The same Reuters memo names Samsung for memory, SanDisk for flash and Sumitomo Electric for fibre optics; Meta has not confirmed these suppliers 21.

7.How it works

SimpleStart here

An MTIA chip is a grid of small processing elements, each with two RISC-V cores and fixed-function maths units, joined by an on-chip network to shared memory 1 3. The early chips kept most of their working data in large on-chip memory and used cheaper LPDDR memory instead of costly HBM, to "reduce cost and power consumption" 2.

DeeperThe detail

MTIA 100 and 200 used an 8 by 8 grid of 64 processing elements 6; MTIA 300 uses a 12 by 6 grid of 72, plus 16 "message engines" for moving data 22. MTIA 300 puts the network cards on the chip: Meta says this made communication 3.9 times faster than an equivalent GPU cluster on a 150-billion-parameter recommendation model 22.

ExpertFor specialists

Software is PyTorch-native: MTIA 200 used PyTorch 2.0 with a Triton-based compiler 6, and the 2026 chips support PyTorch, vLLM, Triton, MLIR and LLVM 3. Meta's recommendation engine for ads retrieval, Andromeda, "can now run on Nvidia, AMD, and MTIA" 13.

9.Official pricing

SimpleStart here

MTIA is not sold or rented. Meta designs the chips for its own data centres only, and publishes no price 4.

ProductPrice
Any MTIA chip or rentalNot published by the company 3
Broadcom partnershipValue not disclosed 5
DeeperThe detail

The only cost figure Meta has published is relative: its ISCA 2025 paper says MTIA 2i "reduces the TCO by an average of 44% compared to GPUs" on Meta's workloads 2. Meta's newsroom calls its custom chips "much more cost efficient" than general-purpose chips for its own work 4.

ExpertFor specialists

Meta's big chip purchases from others carry no public prices either: NVIDIA ("millions" of Blackwell and Rubin GPUs, February 2026) and AMD (up to 6 gigawatts of Instinct GPUs, with a warrant for up to 160 million AMD shares) 7 8. See our NVIDIA and AMD guides for their published cloud prices.

10.The software lock-in

SimpleStart here

Meta's advantage is knowing its own workload. Recommendation models behave differently from chatbots, and Meta designs MTIA around the models that rank content for billions of people 2. It also controls the racks, network and software, and aligns its hardware with Open Compute Project standards 4.

DeeperThe detail

Meta's strategy is flexibility, not replacing NVIDIA. It uses MTIA, NVIDIA, AMD and Arm-based processors side by side 7 8 35, and its ads retrieval engine runs on all three accelerator types 13. Susan Li called custom silicon a source of "long-term strategic flexibility and supply chain leverage" 11.

ExpertFor specialists

Pace is the other lever: four generations in two years, each fitting the same racks 3. Meta has also bought chip talent directly, acquiring RISC-V start-up Rivos in 2025 20.

11.Who buys it and why

SimpleStart here

Meta is MTIA's only user. The chips serve inference for organic content and ads across Meta's apps, and MTIA 300 trains ranking and recommendation models 4 3.

DeeperThe detail

MTIA 2i is "deployed at scale and serving billions of users", according to Meta's 2025 paper 2. Meta has not published what share of its AI work runs on MTIA rather than GPUs 3.

ExpertFor specialists

For training its large language models Meta relies on GPUs: the Hyperion site is described as delivering "over two gigawatts of compute capacity to train future open-source large language models" 25. Press reported in February 2026 that Meta also agreed to rent Google TPUs; neither company confirmed it 36.

12.Hidden in plain sight

SimpleStart here

Public facts buried in Meta's filings and releases. Each links to the exact document.

  1. Meta's annual report never names MTIA 12.
  2. Meta's earnings calls barely mention it either: the July 2026 call referred only to the "internal custom silicon effort" 11.
  3. The chip-design partner's CEO sat on Meta's board: Broadcom's Hock Tan left the board in April 2026 to become an adviser on Meta's chip roadmap 5.
  4. Meta's cheaper chips skipped HBM: the first two generations used laptop-style LPDDR memory to cut cost and power 2.
  5. Capital spending guidance rose during 2026, from $115–135 billion to $125–145 billion, citing "higher component pricing", then to $130–145 billion 15 37 38.
DeeperThe detail
  1. Meta can earn AMD shares for buying AMD chips: a warrant for up to 160 million AMD shares, vesting with GPU shipments 8.
  2. $349 billion of non-cancellable commitments at 30 June 2026 31.
  3. Meta owns only 20% of its biggest AI data centre venture, Hyperion; Blue Owl funds the other 80% 29.
  4. The first MTIA drew just 25 W; press reports put MTIA 500 at 1,700 W 1 32.
  5. Broadcom calls its Meta chip the industry's first 2 nm AI accelerator 23.
ExpertFor specialists
  1. Headcount fell from 78,865 at the end of 2025 to 75,472 in June 2026 while spending soared 12 10.
  2. Depreciation grew 46% year on year in the June 2026 quarter, to $6.356 billion 38.
  3. No 2027 spending guidance yet: "we aren't providing a specific outlook for 2027 CapEx at this time" 11.
  4. Meta's first chip, MSVP, was for video, not AI 16.
  5. Meta's first India data centre is leased from Reliance, not built by Meta 27.

13.Weak spots

SimpleStart here

Limits, from Meta's own statements and reporting:

  • Still dependent on NVIDIA and AMD for training large language models 7 8 25.
  • Setbacks: press reports of a scrapped chip in 2022 and a cancelled training chip in 2026 18 19.
  • Internal only: no outside customers to share the cost 4.
  • Few official numbers for the newest chips; Meta published them only as an image 32.
DeeperThe detail

Meta's newsroom says MTIA 400 to 500 are "capable of handling all workloads" but focused on generative AI inference, with generative AI training secondary 4. Chris Cox described the approach to press in 2025 as "walk, crawl, run" 18.

ExpertFor specialists

The newest chips move from cheaper LPDDR to HBM memory 22 2, giving up one of MTIA's early cost advantages. Meta cited "higher component pricing" when it raised its 2026 spending guidance 37.

14.India angle

SimpleStart here

In June 2026 Meta announced its first data centre in India: a 168 MW site in Jamnagar, Gujarat, built by Reliance and leased by Meta, cooled with desalinated seawater and powered by renewable energy 27. Meta has not said which chips it will use there 27.

DeeperThe detail

Meta has contracted about 1 gigawatt of clean energy in India, from CleanMax (837 MW) and Fourth Partner Energy (88 MW) 39. In October 2025 Reliance and Meta formed an enterprise AI joint venture with an initial ₹855 crore, 70% Reliance and 30% Meta, according to Reliance's exchange filing 40.

ExpertFor specialists

With the IndiaAI Mission, Meta set up the Srijan Centre for Generative AI at IIT Jodhpur and a skilling programme for 1 lakh young people with AICTE 41. No MTIA deployment in India has been disclosed 27.

15.What’s next

SimpleStart here

Only what Meta has announced:

  • MTIA 400: moving toward deployment 3.
  • MTIA 450: mass deployment early 2027 3.
  • MTIA 500: 2027 3.
  • Broadcom: more than 1 gigawatt first, multi-gigawatt later, through 2029 23.
DeeperThe detail

Meta plans a new chip "every six months or less" 4. Reuters reported from an internal memo that production of a chip code-named Iris begins in September 2026 21.

ExpertFor specialists

The test is generative AI. MTIA proved itself on recommendations; the 450 and 500 are aimed at running large generative models 3. Meta's parallel multi-gigawatt GPU orders suggest it is hedging 7 8.
