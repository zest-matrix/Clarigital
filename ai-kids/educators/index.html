Why classroom sessions work well

The Twist works better in groups
When students race to catch AI making a mistake, the competition makes the lesson memorable. Groups catch errors parents and children miss.
Discussion is richer
Questions like "Is AI a tool or a friend?" land differently when 25 students debate them rather than one child and one parent.
No prior knowledge required
You do not need to be an AI expert. The session pages tell you exactly what to say and do. You facilitate — the page teaches.
Immediately relevant
Students are already using AI for homework, social media, and entertainment. These sessions give them language and habits to use it better.

🏆 Best sessions to start with in a classroom

Session 5 — Catch AI Lying

Students race to find mistakes. Competitive, hands-on, immediately shows AI is not infallible. Perfect first classroom session.

Session 4 — Make a Quiz

Students prompt AI to generate a quiz on a topic from their curriculum, then find the errors. Practical and curriculum-connected.

Explorer Session 4 — Debate Partner

AI argues one side of a debate. Students argue the other. Forces critical thinking and shows AI has no real opinions.

Builder Session 4 — AI Bias

Finding bias in AI outputs. Generates the most discussion of any session. Best for ages 11+.
