Common questions

Do we have to do the programmes in order?
Yes for the main programmes — Starter builds the foundation that Explorer and Builder rely on. Monthly sessions are entirely independent and can be done in any order alongside or after the main programmes.
Can we skip Starter if my child already uses AI?
If your child uses AI casually but has no structured learning, still start with Starter — it builds the safety habits more than the technical knowledge. If they have done structured AI learning before, you can begin at Explorer.
How long does the full path take?
At 2 sessions per week: Starter (4 weeks) + Explorer (8 weeks) + Builder (12 weeks) = about 6 months. Add Monthly as an ongoing cadence alongside or after. There is no deadline — go at whatever pace works.
What if we miss sessions or take a break?
Nothing happens. There are no deadlines, no cohorts, no expiry. Your progress is saved in your browser. Come back whenever you are ready.
Is there a certificate at the end?
Each programme ends with a graduation session. Starter Session 8, Explorer Session 16, and Builder Session 24 are all graduation sessions — they review everything and celebrate completion.
