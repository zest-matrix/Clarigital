Session 7 of 24 — AI Builder

AI Builder · Session 7 of 24⏱ 30 min · 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent aware

🗣️ AI for Communication

Write better emails, messages, and presentations.

Stuck at any point? Ask an AI about this page →
🎯 Today's goal: Your child uses AI to improve their own writing — learning that AI suggests, they decide, and the final voice must be theirs.

🔥 Warm Up — 3 minutes

Warm Up

"Communication is one of the most valuable skills there is. AI can help you communicate more clearly and professionally. But it can also make your writing sound like everyone else's."

"Today we learn to use AI to improve communication — without losing your own voice."

🤖 The Activity — 20 minutes

Main Activity

Real communication tasks. Real improvements. Your child's voice stays theirs.

Task 1 — Improve without replacing

Your child writes a short email (3-4 sentences) asking a teacher for an extension on an assignment. Their own words, their own style.

📋 Type this exactly
Here is an email I wrote: [paste child's email]. Make it more polite and professional without changing what I am asking for. Keep it feeling like it was written by a student, not an adult.

Compare the two. What changed? What stayed the same? Did it lose their voice? Revise together until it sounds like them — but better.

Task 2 — Explain a complex idea simply

📋 Type this exactly
I need to explain [something your child learned recently — from school, from this programme, from a hobby] to someone who knows nothing about it. My explanation needs to be: under 60 words, use no jargon, and include one analogy. Help me write it.

After AI drafts it: your child reads it aloud. Does it sound like them? Do they actually believe every word? Revise anything that does not feel right.

🧩 The Twist — 5 minutes

Deep Dive
📋 Type this exactly
Write a generic version of the email I wrote — the kind of email any student anywhere might send. Then show me what makes mine different and more personal.

This makes visible what personalisation actually is. Ask: Which would a teacher rather receive? Why does the personal version work better? The lesson: AI can improve your writing, but the personal details that make it yours come only from you.

🛡️ Safety Moment
“Don't use AI to do your homework”

There is a fine line between using AI to improve your communication and using AI to replace it. The test: read the final version aloud. Every word should feel true to you. If there are sentences you would never say — cut them. The writing is yours when you can defend every choice in it.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent: read this aloud. Ask your child to repeat it back.

Parent Notes — tap to expand

▼
What they learned

Communication and voice — using AI as an editor rather than a ghostwriter. The distinction between improving and replacing is one of the most important in the programme.

Questions to ask
  • What changed in your email after AI improved it?
  • How do you know when AI has gone too far and the writing no longer sounds like you?
  • What is something AI could never write for you because it does not know your life?
What to watch for

Some children find it liberating to use AI to improve their writing. Others feel uncomfortable — like it is not fully theirs anymore. Both reactions are valid and worth exploring.

Safety in context

The ghostwriting concern is real in schools right now. The principle established here — AI as editor, not author — is the most defensible position for students. They can improve their writing with AI and still genuinely own what they submit.
