What this builds

Not AI skills.
Thinking skills.

The sessions use AI as the subject matter. The actual skills are something else entirely.

🧠

Critical thinking

The habit of asking "how do I check this?" before believing what AI — or anyone — tells them.

🎯

Scepticism without cynicism

Knowing AI makes mistakes without dismissing everything. Understanding what it is good at and bad at.

✍️

Precise communication

Learning that clearer questions get better answers — a skill that transfers to every conversation.

🔍

Research rigour

Verifying facts, tracing claims to sources, understanding why "AI said so" is never enough.

💡

Creative confidence

Using AI as a collaborator without letting it replace their own thinking and voice.

🛡

Safe independence

The instincts and habits to use AI tools throughout their life without being manipulated by them.

Before you ask

The things parents
want to know.

💰

Why is this free?

Clarigital is an education platform. Everything on it — 534 marketing guides, 80 AI tool guides, this curriculum — is free. No ad model. No subscription. No catch. We believe this information should be accessible.

🔒

What data is collected?

Nothing from your child. Sessions run entirely in your browser. There are no child accounts, no logins, no forms. Progress is saved locally on your device. We never see it.

👨‍👩‍👧

Do I need to know about AI?

No. You learn alongside your child. The Parent Notes on each session page tell you exactly what concept is being taught, what questions to ask, and what to watch for. You are not a teacher — you are a co-learner.

📱

What device do we need?

Any device with a browser — laptop, tablet, or phone. A laptop or tablet is easiest so you can sit side by side. Your child should never use the device alone during sessions.

🤖

Which AI tool do we use?

ChatGPT (free, no login needed for basic use) or Claude.ai. You use your own account — never create one in your child's name. The sessions work with either tool.

⏸️

What if we miss sessions?

Nothing happens. There are no deadlines, no cohorts, no expiry. Come back whenever you are ready. Your progress is saved. The content will be here.

Safety

The 5 rules —
reinforced in every session.

One rule per session. Repeated until it is automatic. Read the full Safety Guide →

🔴

Never share personal information

No real name, school, age, city, address, phone number, or photos — ever. Not even when AI asks.

🟠

AI makes mistakes — always check

It sounds confident either way. The habit of asking "how would I check this?" is a life skill.

🟡

AI is a tool, not a friend

It has no feelings, no knowledge of your child, and no care for them. Real relationships are with people.

🔵

Don't use AI to do your homework

Use it to understand your homework. The difference is between learning and copying.

🟣

If it feels wrong, close the tab and tell a grown-up

No explanation needed. No questions asked. Just close it. Always.
