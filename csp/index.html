How It Works
Simple. Daily. Structured.
01

Register on Friday

Fill in the registration form for your chosen program. Get your unique Student ID instantly. New batches start every Monday.

02

Read the day's content

Each day has a structured page with an explainer, real examples, and a link to a full Clarigital guide to read.

03

Pass the quiz

5 questions per day. Need 3 out of 5 to unlock submission. Unlimited retries. Instant feedback on every answer.

04

Submit your task

Write and submit a short task applying what you learned. Tasks are specific and real — not generic exercises.

05

Each page opens once

Every task page is locked to a specific day of the week. Monday's task opens Monday. This keeps the cohort together.

06

Complete = Certificate

Submit all days on time and your certificate with your unique Student ID is confirmed. No exam required.

Clarigital runs this program because we believe students who genuinely understand digital marketing have a real advantage. The website is free. The program is free. The certificate is real.
