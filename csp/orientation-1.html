🙋 Who Is This For?

You don't need to know anything to start. Seriously. You don't need a marketing degree. You don't need to have run a campaign. You don't need to understand what SEO or Google Ads means yet.

If you've ever seen an ad on Instagram, searched something on Google, or received a promotional email — you've already experienced digital marketing as a consumer. This program teaches you how it works from the other side.

📋 How the Program Works

Every day, a new page opens. You read, you take a quick quiz, you write a short task and submit. That's it. Each page takes 20–30 minutes.

01

Each task page opens on a specific weekday

Day 1 is Monday. Day 2 is Tuesday. Day 6 is Saturday. Sunday is always a rest day — no tasks. The page is locked before and after its assigned day.

02

Read the explainer and the linked Clarigital guide

Each day has a 300–500 word explainer written specifically for that day. There's also a link to the full Clarigital guide on that topic — read it. That's your reference material.

03

Pass the quiz — 3 out of 5

5 multiple choice questions. You need 3 correct to unlock the submission form. Unlimited retries. Every answer gives you instant feedback explaining why it's right or wrong.

04

Write and submit your task

Each task asks you to apply the day's learning to a real scenario. Not a summary. Not "explain what you learned." A specific, practical output — something you could actually use.

05

Your CSP ID is your identity

Use it to submit every task. You can also refer friends — anyone who registers using your referral link is tracked to you.

💡
Think of it like a daily newspaper, not a textbook. You don't read the whole thing at once. One page, one day, one topic. By the end of 14 days, you've read the whole edition — and you've actually retained it because you wrote something each day.

❤️ Being Honest With You

Why is this free? What's the catch?

There is no catch. Clarigital is a free digital marketing and AI reference website. This program exists as an extension of that — structured, daily learning that drives students through the guides we've already written.

You invest your time (20–25 mins/day). We invest the content. Your certificate is real — because it required daily work, quiz passes, and actual written submissions to earn it. That's what makes it worth something.

This is an unpaid internship. There are no fees and no payments on either side.

💡Clarigital is your textbook throughout this program. Every day links to a specific guide on the site. Bookmark clarigital.com — you'll be using it constantly.
