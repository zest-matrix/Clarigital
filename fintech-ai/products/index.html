How to use this page

Twenty-one guides, each taking one product from nothing to live. This page is for finding the right one.

It is organised by what you are trying to do rather than by product name, because a reader who already knows the product name does not need a front door — they need the grid, which is further down.

Note

Every guide follows the same shape: eight steps, the options at each one, what goes in and what comes out, what it costs, and what breaks. Each one names the regulator whose rules govern it and dates every claim. Where something is genuinely unsettled the page says so rather than inventing a position — there is one draft in the set and it is labelled as one.

Three questions first

Three questions worth answering before you pick one:

QuestionWhy it decides which guide
Are you regulated, or building for someone who is?Almost every guide has a different shape depending on which side of that line you sit
Are you holding money at any point?Holding funds, even briefly, changes what licence you need. See Licensing and Access
Do you need the data, or the decision?Several guides exist because teams built the first when they needed the second
Note

If you do not yet have the keys for any of this, start at Getting Access — which APIs are self-serve today, which need a sales call, and which are gated behind a licence.

Watch out

These guides are written to be built from and are not specifications. Indian financial regulation changes by notification, sometimes between one of these sessions and the next. Every page dates its claims and names its sources; check them before you rely on anything. Nothing here is legal advice.

Sources

This page is a route map rather than a source of claims. Each guide it points at carries its own dated, typed sources.
