What this is

One dated record of what changed and when, across every product guide on this site.

It exists because regulation is the part of a build that moves after you have finished. A guide tells you the rule as at the day it was written; this tells you when the rule arrived, and what is still coming.

Note

Every entry links to the guide that relies on it, where the instrument is named, dated and typed by source. Nothing here is a summary of the law — it is an index of when things moved, with a route to the page that explains each one.

Watch out

The date to circle is 1 January 2027. Three separate instruments commence that day — IRDAI’s named-seller requirement, the RBI Responsible Business Conduct Second Amendment on bundling and dark patterns, and the Fourth Amendment on recovery conduct. Anyone touching insurance distribution or collections has one deadline for three unrelated pieces of work, and they were notified months apart.
