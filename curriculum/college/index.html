College Curriculum

A twelve-module curriculum for undergraduate and postgraduate study of digital marketing and artificial intelligence in professional practice. Designed for university adoption globally. All readings link to official, verified primary sources.

Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this curriculum, students will be able to:

  • Analyse the architecture of the contemporary digital marketing ecosystem and evaluate the strategic role of each channel within an integrated marketing framework.
  • Apply search engine optimisation principles to audit, diagnose and improve the organic search performance of a website or content property.
  • Design and critically evaluate paid media campaigns across search and social platforms, with demonstrable competence in budget allocation, targeting strategy and performance measurement.
  • Develop a data-driven content strategy that integrates editorial planning, SEO requirements and audience development objectives.
  • Construct and evaluate marketing automation workflows that connect email marketing, CRM systems and cross-channel campaign execution.
  • Apply appropriate AI tools to professional marketing tasks across writing, image creation, research, data analysis and campaign planning, with critical awareness of each tool's capabilities and limitations.
  • Design a comprehensive digital marketing campaign integrating multiple channels, with a coherent measurement framework and attribution model.
  • Evaluate the implications of AI-driven transformation for marketing team structures, skill requirements and professional practice.
Academic integrity and source standards: All reading materials in this curriculum link directly to official platform documentation, peer-reviewed research, or verified primary sources. Clarigital does not produce proprietary research — it organises, verifies and explains knowledge that belongs to its original sources. Every guide cites its primary sources explicitly. There are no paywalls, no affiliate links and no advertising. This curriculum is free to use for any educational or institutional purpose.

How to use this curriculum

1
Follow the module sequence

Modules are ordered from strategic foundations through to specialist applications. Sequential study produces the strongest conceptual framework.

2
The links are the required reading

Every guide linked within a module is essential reading for that module — not supplementary. Each links to official primary sources.

3
Allow 2–3 hours per module

Reading time is 2–3 hours per module. Allow additional time for notes and reflection. Total curriculum: approximately 27 hours of reading.

4
Use the assessment bank for evaluation

A separate assessment bank provides MCQs, short answer and essay questions for every module — suitable for assignments, exams or self-assessment.

🎓 Self-study: 1–2 modules per week — 6–12 weeks 🏫 Classroom: one module per 2-hour session — 12 sessions 🏢 Corporate training: one session per week — 3 months

Agentic AI in Marketing Context

Agentic AI represents the next significant development in AI for marketing professionals. Rather than a marketer prompting an AI tool for each discrete output, agentic systems can be given a goal — conduct competitor analysis, draft a campaign brief, compile a performance report — and execute the required steps autonomously. Understanding the architecture, capabilities, and limitations of these systems is increasingly necessary for marketing leaders making tool investment and workflow decisions.

Key concepts for marketing professionals: the orchestrator/specialist agent model mirrors how marketing teams are structured; RAG-based agents can reason over proprietary brand assets and customer data; the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic AI (2026) documents the security risks relevant to organisations deploying automated workflows; and the EU AI Act high-risk classifications take full effect August 2026, with compliance obligations for certain agentic deployments.

Current production use cases in marketing: automated research and competitive intelligence pipelines, multi-step content production workflows, customer data analysis and reporting, campaign performance monitoring with automated alert and triage logic. Recommended frameworks for marketing teams evaluating agentic tools: CrewAI for role-based multi-agent workflows; LangGraph for complex conditional pipelines; LlamaIndex for knowledge base and document-heavy applications.
