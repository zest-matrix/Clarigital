3.The story

  • 1997 The Taiwan registry’s establishment date for 十銓科技股份有限公司 corresponds to 9 April 1997 7.
  • 2018 Team Research, Inc. files, and later registers, the U.S. “TEAMGROUP” word mark 8.

DeeperThe detail

The three trademark filings, read together, sketch a company that has been actively managing its U.S. brand protection rather than letting it lapse. Unlike this section’s G.Skill and ADATA guides — where the pattern was marks quietly cancelled for missed Section 8 renewal filings years after registration — TeamGroup’s two live marks (“TEAMGROUP” and “TF”) both show active maintenance: the TEAMGROUP mark’s Section 8 declaration was accepted as recently as 9 April 2025, and TSDR’s own prosecution history for the TF mark shows a courtesy reminder for its six-year Section 8 filing emailed 16 February 2026 — ahead of the registration’s actual six-year deadline, which falls around February 2027 8 2. The one lapse in this guide’s trademark record is different in kind: the “TF T-FORCE” filing never reached registration at all, abandoned after the applicant did not respond to an office action — a new-application failure, not a maintenance failure on an existing right 9.

ExpertFor specialists

No source read for this guide explains why a fresh “TF T-FORCE” application was filed in May 2025 when the company already held a live “TF” design mark since 2021 and a live “TEAMGROUP” word mark since 2018 — possibly to cover the specific combined wordmark-plus-design form, possibly for another reason entirely. This page does not speculate; it reports the filing and its abandonment exactly as TSDR shows them 2 9.

DeeperThe detail

The JEDEC and AMD EXPO absences are worth reading as genuine negative findings rather than gaps in this check’s research. This does not mean TeamGroup memory is incompatible with either standard; JEDEC membership and an EXPO partnership are commercial/organizational relationships a company may simply not have entered, not requirements for a product to work.

13.Weak spots

SimpleStart here

Four weak spots, each one a limit of what this check could establish rather than a claim about the company itself.

  • No specification for any product. Not one timing, voltage or part number was found on any reachable page 3.
  • No chip-supplier disclosure. Not named on any page reachable from this environment.
  • The main storefront and industrial sub-brand are both unreachable. Everything beyond the support portal could not be checked 3 4.
  • The stock listing could not be independently confirmed. Stock code 4967 appears on multiple financial-data mirrors, but neither TWSE’s own lookup nor TPEx’s own API confirms it from an official source this session 10 11.
DeeperThe detail

The warranty policy is the one genuinely rich document this guide could read in full, and it deserves a careful summary rather than a bare mention. It states plainly that “the warranty period of TEAMGROUP’s product starts from the date of the purchase” and that “customers must present Invoices or shipping documents” when claiming warranty — quoted here exactly as published, typo included 5. Coverage varies sharply by product: memory modules carry a Limited Lifetime Warranty; USB flash drives range from one to five years or Limited Lifetime depending on model; memory cards range from two to five years or Limited Lifetime depending on model, with no one-year tier; SSDs range from one to twelve years or a TBW (terabytes-written) based limit depending on model; mobile accessories carry one year; and AIO liquid coolers are covered for component replacement only 5. The policy also states explicitly, in the same language used for memory, USB drives and memory cards alike, that “this lifetime warranty might not apply to customers in certain countries or states due to conflict of laws” 5.

Ten numbered exclusions (5.1–5.10) void coverage for misuse, accidents, unauthorized modification, computer viruses, electromagnetic interference or unstable power or static electricity, improper operating environments, transportation damage, data loss, R&D-only usage, and a tampered or removed warranty label — a materially more detailed exclusion list than this section’s other thin-built guides could retrieve for their respective brands 5.

ExpertFor specialists

One further limit, stated for completeness. The stock-code figure cited above (4967) rests on convergent third-party financial mirrors this site’s own standing discipline treats as uncitable on their own — the same discipline applied to Goodinfo, HiStock and similar sites in this section’s ADATA and G.Skill guides. It is reported here only as a fact this check could not confirm from an official source, not as a verified listing detail.
