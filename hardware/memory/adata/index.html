5.How a module is built

SimpleStart here

No page reachable from this environment describes step-by-step how an ADATA module or drive is assembled, tested or binned. ADATA Industrial’s own page names three quality and reliability standards its products are built to meet — AEC-Q100, IATF 16949 and ISO/SAE 21434 — without describing the manufacturing process itself 3.

DeeperThe detail

Those three standards are worth naming precisely, since each covers a different part of the manufacturing and quality picture rather than the product itself. AEC-Q100 is an Automotive Electronics Council stress-test qualification for integrated circuits; IATF 16949 is an automotive-sector quality-management-system standard, describing how a manufacturer runs its processes rather than a property of any one part; and ISO/SAE 21434 is a road-vehicle cybersecurity engineering standard 3. ADATA Industrial’s own page names all three as standards its products meet, without stating which specific product lines carry which certification, or linking a certificate for independent verification 3.

ExpertFor specialists

This section is left this short deliberately. ADATA’s own Intellectual Property Report separately states the company operates manufacturing facilities in three locations — covered in the next section, since that is a where-question, not a how-question — but no source read for this guide describes the assembly, screening or binning process itself for any ADATA product line 7. Nothing here should be read as a claim that ADATA does not publish this; only that this check could not find it among the pages read.

The confectionery trademark is the most surprising single fact this guide could confirm, precisely because it is easy to verify and easy to misread. ExpertFor specialists

What makes ADATA’s case worth a separate callout is not the lapse itself but that a secondary database (Justia) reported a status this guide’s own direct TSDR check found to be stale for both marks checked, which is why every trademark claim on this page is sourced to TSDR directly rather than to Justia’s listing, wherever both are available 8 9.
