Corsair Dominator Platinum RGB DDR5: The Complete Guide

Corsair’s RGB DDR5, launched on 27 October 2021 alongside Vengeance DDR5, with Dominator kits reaching 6,400MHz by December 2021 and 6,600MT/s by May 2022. Simple to deep dive, every number links to its source.

1.At a glance

SimpleStart here

Dominator Platinum RGB DDR5 is Corsair’s DDR5 desktop memory with RGB lighting and DHX cooling. Corsair launched it on 27 October 2021 alongside Vengeance DDR5, saying the DDR5 launch was at “speeds of up to 5,200MHz” 1. By May 2022 Corsair was announcing 6,600MT/s kits 2.

Deep diveThe technical detail

The release is datelined “FREMONT, CA, October 27th, 2021” and covers Dominator Platinum RGB DDR5 together with Vengeance DDR5 1. A second release dated December 16th, 2021 announces 6,200MHz and 6,400MHz kits 3, and a third dated May 31st, 2022 announces speeds up to 6,600MT/s 2. Today Corsair’s store sells the line with Intel XMP-only and AMD EXPO & Intel XMP kits 45.

2.Launch and lineup

SimpleStart here

Corsair’s December 2021 release says the line’s patented DHX cooling design ensures performance is not held back at 6,400MHz 3.

Deep diveThe technical detail

The October 2021 release says Dominator Platinum RGB DDR5 is “lined with 12 ultra-bright individually addressable CAPELLIX LEDs per module” and that “With its patented CORSAIR DHX cooling design, DOMINATOR PLATINUM RGB DDR5 delivers exceptional performance” 1. The December release describes an overclocking result that “set a new record level of speed for CORSAIR DDR5 memory by reaching a speed of 8,149MT/s” 3. This guide reports it as Corsair’s claim.

3.What’s inside the module

SimpleStart here

Each module carries 12 CAPELLIX RGB LEDs, and Corsair’s iCUE software controls the lighting 16.

Deep diveThe technical detail

Corsair’s explainer page says “With world-renowned design lit by 12 ultra-bright CAPELLIX RGB LEDs” and that iCUE software “enables RGB lighting customization and synchronization” 6. The current store lists the heat spreader as anodized aluminum, in grey on the EXPO kit page 5. Neither the 6200MT/s nor the 5200MT/s product page states module dimensions 45.

4.Full spec table

SimpleStart here

At the DDR5 launch (Dominator and Vengeance): up to 5,200MHz and 32GB per module, with Dominator kits of 32GB to 64GB 1. By May 2022: up to 6,600MT/s at 32-39-39-76 in 2x16GB kits 2. Today’s store shows examples at 5200 and 6200MT/s 54.

5.Where it’s made

SimpleStart here

Corsair’s pages checked for this guide do not name a DRAM die supplier 124.

Deep diveThe technical detail

The product pages list an “Overclock PMIC” as the PMIC type but name no maker, and none of the Corsair sources used here names the DRAM chip maker 4512. The explainer page says only “Hand-sorted, tightly screened memory chips” 6.

6.What it’s compatible with

SimpleStart here

The 2021 release names Intel XMP 3.0. The current store has kits marked Intel XMP only and kits marked AMD EXPO & Intel XMP 145.

Deep diveThe technical detail

The October 2021 release says iCUE enables you to “customize and save your own Intel® XMP 3.0 profiles”, with the feature “coming later in 2021 to select motherboards” 1. The 5200MT/s kit’s heading and Performance Profile row read “AMD EXPO & Intel XMP” and lists AMD 600 and 800 Series and Intel 600, 700 and 800 Series for compatibility 5. The 6200MT/s kit’s page reads “Intel XMP” with no EXPO row 4.

7.Official pricing

SimpleStart here

Corsair’s store lists $643.99 for the 6200MT/s 32GB kit and $542.99 for the 5200MT/s EXPO 32GB kit 45.

Deep diveThe technical detail

Those are the prices on the two checked product pages in October 2026 and they change 45. Each release sends readers to Corsair’s website for pricing and states none itself 132.

8.Rated vs. real-world speed

SimpleStart here

Corsair publishes rated speeds and timings, not benchmarks. This guide ran none. The line carries a limited lifetime warranty 1.

Deep diveThe technical detail

The May 2022 release warns that “Not all 12th Generation Intel® Core™ Processors support DDR5 frequencies of 6,600MHz” and recommends “A top-binned Intel CORE i9-12900K” to reach it 2. The releases state a limited lifetime warranty 132; the terms are in Corsair’s warranty article 7.

9.Where this family sits in the lineup

SimpleStart here

Corsair released it with Vengeance DDR5 in the same 27 October 2021 announcement 1.

Deep diveThe technical detail

The October 2021 release covers Dominator Platinum RGB DDR5 and Vengeance DDR5 together 1. None of the Corsair sources checked for this guide names a successor or a predecessor 1326.

10.Hidden in plain sight

SimpleStart here

The launch release names only Intel XMP 3.0, but Corsair’s store now sells kits marked AMD EXPO & Intel XMP 15.

Deep diveThe technical detail

The 2021 release says iCUE can customise “Intel® XMP 3.0” profiles and mentions no AMD profile 1; the May 2022 release mentions iCUE “with XMP Manager Preview” and no AMD profile 2. The current 5200MT/s kit page’s heading and Performance Profile row read “AMD EXPO & Intel XMP”, and it says “Optimized timings, speeds, and voltages are available for speedy overclocking with Intel XMP and AMD EXPO” 5, while the 6200MT/s kit stays Intel XMP only 4. Corsair dates none of this change, so which kits gained EXPO and when is not stated 54.
