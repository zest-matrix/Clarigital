Technical SEO

Technical SEO is the work that makes content findable, indexable and fast. Great content on a technically broken site does not rank, which is why this comes first in any audit.

What Technical SEO Covers

Four broad areas. Whether search engines can reach your pages — crawling, robots directives, site architecture. Whether they can index them — canonicals, duplicate handling, rendering. How well the pages perform — Core Web Vitals, load speed, mobile. And how clearly they communicate meaning — structured data, hreflang, semantic markup.

Where to Start

Work in order of dependency. A page that cannot be crawled will never be indexed, and an unindexed page cannot be slow or fast in any way that matters. Begin with crawlability and indexation, then duplication, then rendering, then performance, then structured data.

The audit guide in this section sets out that sequence in full.
