Local Citations and NAP Consistency

A citation is any online mention of your business name, address and phone number. Consistency across them is a prominence signal — and inconsistency is a common, fixable drag on local rankings.

What Counts as a Citation

Structured citations are directory listings — Yelp, Yellow Pages, Apple Maps, Bing Places, industry directories, chamber of commerce listings. They follow a fixed format with dedicated NAP fields.

Unstructured citations are mentions in ordinary content — a local news article, a sponsorship page, a blog post about the area. These carry weight too, and often more, because they are harder to manufacture.

Why Consistency Matters

Search engines cross-reference your business details across many sources to build confidence that the business exists as described. Conflicting data undermines that confidence.

The common causes are mundane: an office move that was never updated everywhere, a rebrand, a changed phone number, a listing created by an employee years ago, or an aggregator that propagated an old address to dozens of directories.

Abbreviations count. "Street" and "St" are treated as different by some systems. Pick one format and use it everywhere.

Auditing Existing Citations

Search your business name, phone number, and address separately in quotes. Each surfaces a different set of listings. Phone number searches in particular find listings you have forgotten about.

Build a spreadsheet: directory, URL, listed NAP, correct or incorrect, claimed or unclaimed, action needed. This is tedious and it is the work.

Pay attention to the data aggregators that feed many smaller directories. Correcting an aggregator fixes downstream listings you would otherwise chase individually.

Priorities and Realism

Not all citations are equal. Focus effort on the major platforms, the significant industry directories in your sector, and genuinely local sources. A listing on an abandoned directory nobody visits contributes almost nothing.

Citation building has diminishing returns. Once the important platforms are accurate and consistent, additional low-quality listings do not help. Services offering hundreds of citations for a small fee are selling volume that does not convert into ranking.
