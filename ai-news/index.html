How this page works

The brief is written every morning at 6:00 AM IST by an AI (Claude) that searches the news. Every story links to the report it came from, and any story whose link did not come from that search is removed automatically before publishing. It is not checked by a Clarigital editor, so open the source before relying on a detail. The live section comes straight from the companies’ own news feeds. Nothing about you is stored.

Where this page gets its news

Checked September 2026. Company feeds are the companies’ own publications.

  • OpenAI News feed: openai.com/news
  • Google blog feed (AI stories only): blog.google
  • Google DeepMind blog feed: deepmind.google/blog
  • Microsoft Source feed (AI stories only): news.microsoft.com/source
  • NVIDIA Newsroom feed: nvidianews.nvidia.com
  • Hugging Face blog feed: huggingface.co/blog
  • Anthropic, Meta AI, xAI, Mistral and DeepSeek do not publish a news feed. Their cards show stories discussed on Hacker News (search API), labelled as discussion, not as the company’s own words. Their own newsrooms are linked on each card.
  • TechCrunch AI section feed: techcrunch.com
  • The daily brief: written with the Claude API web search tool; each story links to its own source.
